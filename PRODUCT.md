# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior business and technical decision-makers who need a credible partner for consequential software, AI, security, and platform work. They arrive under delivery pressure and need to judge both technical depth and operating maturity quickly.

## Product Purpose

Juliani Consulting Company presents an independent software consultancy for difficult work that must survive real operating conditions. The site should make a serious first impression, establish technical authority, and make starting a conversation straightforward.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, technical. The tone is calm under pressure, fluent in complex systems, and visibly intentional rather than performative.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, neon-hacker visual language, decorative AI effects, and motion that is disconnected from the identity it is meant to express.

## Design Principles

- Make the system legible before making it ornamental.
- Use motion to explain identity and state, not to fill space.
- Let technical depth read as control, not visual noise.
- Make important transitions feel deliberate and inevitable.
- Give decision-makers a fast path from interest to a concrete conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by landing immediately on a readable final identity. Maintain strong text contrast and avoid making color or motion the sole carrier of information.
